About us

We are a small, family-run campsite built on one idea: sleep close to nature without giving up a good night’s rest. Our grounds are shaded by old trees and shared with wildlife and birds.

Whether you want a ready-made tent with a proper bed or just a patch of ground for your own, you are welcome. Our team is on site daily to help with firewood, directions and travel tips.

On site

  • Shared ablution blocks with hot showers
  • Fire pits and firewood on request
  • Braai and picnic areas
  • Walking and birdwatching trails
  • Safe parking next to your site
